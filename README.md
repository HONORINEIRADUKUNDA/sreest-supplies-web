# Sreest Paints & Supplies Ltd — Corporate Supply & Procurement Portal

Official commercial web portal for **Sreest Paints and Supplies Ltd**, located at the Lesso Building, Gikondo, Kicukiro, Kigali, Rwanda.

## Core Supply & Procurement Divisions
- **Solar & Lighting**: Solar street lights, waterproof LED floodlights, and decorative interior fixtures.
- **Electricals**: Copper wiring rolls, power cables, wall sockets, switches, and circuit breakers.
- **Paints & Construction Tools**: Interior/exterior architectural paints, primers, waterproofing coatings, and hardware tools.
- **Generators & Backup Power**: Heavy-duty diesel and petrol generators for commercial, industrial, and residential use.
- **Electric Mobility (EVs)**: Zero-emission Electric Cars and heavy-duty delivery Electric Bikes.
- **Custom Bulk LPO Procurement**: Factory-direct sourcing for office, hospitality, and institutional supplies delivered within 7 days (DDP).

## Corporate Terms & Banking
- **Delivery**: DDP straight to site within 1 week of LPO.
- **Payment**: Cash on Delivery (COD) discounts with official EBM 2.1 VAT tax invoices.
- **Equity Bank Accounts**: RWF (`4012201358599`) and USD (`4012201418645`).
